Buying of AI names, but can it build?

  • Chris Weston |
  • chris.weston@pepperstone.com |
  • 00:57

Following Anthropic's presentation to business leaders showcasing its impressive product range, the broad-based gains across the AI ecosystem should be taken as a positive, as the risk of a further AI scare and further rotation was elevated. Over the past two weeks, the raft of new AI products has created anxieties not just in software, but across multiple touchpoints within US enterprise. It is therefore constructive that we are seeing gains across enablers, AI “losers”, and infrastructure stocks. Whether this momentum can extend is what we will be watching closely.

US equity investors have recently built up portfolio protection and ramped up downsdie hedges - We have seen increasing relative demand for downside puts versus calls, meaning investors do not necessarily need to reduce equity exposure given the hedging structures in place. However, the levels are clearly defined, and an upside break of these trading ranges could encourage further buying and potentially a push toward all-time highs.
