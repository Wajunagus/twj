YOU KNOW WHAT TO DO… SO WHY DON'T YOU DO IT?

Most of us know the basics of good trading behaviour.

  • We know we shouldn't chase.
  • We know we shouldn't revenge trade.
  • We know we should follow our risk rules and stick to the plan.

And then the market opens…

…and somehow it all goes to pot.

That's the interesting bit.

Because the problem often isn't a lack of knowledge.

It's the gap between knowing what we should do and actually being able to do it when there's money on the line, uncertainty everywhere, and emotions starting to creep into the decision.

So tonight we're going to dig into that gap.

We'll look at why our decision-making can change once we're in the heat of the market, how to start identifying our own behavioural risks, where they tend to show up, and early warning signs that we might be starting to make some pretty poor decisions.

The goal over the whole series isn't to somehow become an emotionless trading robot…

It's to understand your trading brain better…

What tends to trigger you personally, and, perhaps more importantly… what practical rules, routines, and circuit breakers you can put around that.

Tonight, 7 pm UK time.
