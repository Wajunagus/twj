We've all been there…

You've spotted a nice setup, got your levels marked out, but for whatever reason, you miss the entry.

Maybe you hesitated, maybe the trigger didn't quite fire, or maybe you just didn't see it until after the fact.

And then off it goes… straight in your direction.

Now you're sitting there watching all that lovely profit you could have been banking.

“Damn, why did I miss this? Well, I want to make sure I won't miss any more of it!”

So you click, you're in the trade… and of course, typically that's when the market reverses.

That's FOMO for you…

THE TRADE'S GONE… WHY ARE YOU STILL CHASING IT?

We'll look at why that urge to chase can be so powerful, when we're most vulnerable to it, and some practical ways to stop it from getting the better of us.

Better entry planning, alerts, a few simple rules, plus a training drill to help you catch yourself before you make that impulsive click.
