Train Your Trading Brain with Mark Holstead

Join Mark Holstead for Train Your Trading Brain, a 12-week series exploring the behavioural side of trading - the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it.

Each week, Mark focuses on a specific behavioural challenge traders commonly face - from FOMO and revenge trading to hesitation, overtrading, recency bias and P&L-driven decisions - looking at what triggers it, when you're most susceptible, and the practical adjustments to strategy, environment and process that can help you manage it.

By the final session, you'll have built your own Trading Brain Profile: a personal record of your key behavioural risks, triggers, warning signs, operating rules and circuit breakers.
