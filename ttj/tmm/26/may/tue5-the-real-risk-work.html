Let's talk about risk for a moment…

And I don't mean the usual stuff like position size, stop losses, and the 1% rule.

I mean, how do you think about risk as an allocator of capital…

Huh?!

CAPITAL ALLOCATION

Now, that phrase might sound quite grand for a retail trader.

But whether you're trading with £500 or £500,000, you're still allocating capital to ideas.

Scalping? Same. Swing trading? Same.

We're all running a small capital allocation business…

The basics still apply, of course. Stops, circuit breakers, live to fight another day.

But those are the last lines of defence. The fail-safe, if you like.

No one wants to be bouncing off their max daily loss every other day; recovery takes too long, financially and mentally…

The real risk work is upstream of all that.

  • It's regime risk, like sizing 5R into a single thesis without realising.
  • It's thinking in odds, even when you're a discretionary trader.
  • It's knowing what job each piece of capital you allocate to a trade is actually doing.

The idea being, if you build a good relationship with risk now, regardless of your account size… When you do size up in the future, you'll be as well-equipped as possible.

And so in tonight's Pepperstone webinar, that's what we are going to be focusing on.
