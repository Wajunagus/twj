Half of South Korea's stock market value rests on just two companies

  • CNBC TV 18 |
  • Tue, Jun 02, '26 14:58 GMT+3 |
  • 2 min read

Chipmakers are reshaping global equity rankings, underscoring investors' growing conviction that the biggest winners of the AI revolution will be the companies supplying its critical infrastructure.

In South Korea, the rally has been driven largely by semiconductor giants Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, both of which recently crossed the $1 trillion valuation mark. In Taiwan, the market's fortunes are even more tightly linked to Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), the world's dominant advanced chip manufacturer.
