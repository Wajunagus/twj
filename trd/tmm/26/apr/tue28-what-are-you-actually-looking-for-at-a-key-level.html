Reading price behaviour around key levels is the make-or-break part of any trading playbook.

All the work you've done prior leads up to this moment.

Conditions, regime, higher TF analysis, any indicators you use…

Now it's 'pull the trigger or don't' time.

So, what are we actually looking for to initiate a trade?

READING PRICE BEHAVIOUR

I see this as three options:

  1. Simple and rule-based: e.g., if price > this level then buy
  2. Mechanical from a menu: e.g., engulfing, ignition, 123 patterns
  3. A blend of everything with discretion layered in…

Because what we're really reading is supply vs demand… and the shift that triggers execution.

If price comes ripping up to a major key level with a final exhaustion-style candle and starts to stall? We'd probably both be all over that in a heartbeat.

But grinding up and flirting with the level? We'd likely want something extra.
