Risk, risk, risk…

Three little words every trader learns very quickly 🙂

So, most of us pick up the standard risk rules early on…

“Stop is 100 points away, I want to risk £1,000 on the trade, my size is £10 per point.”

That's fine, that's the basics…

But once you move beyond that, risk stops being just for protection…It becomes a tool.

A lever you can adjust up or down, depending on the situation in front of you…

RISK, RISK, RISK

Because we all know these things to be true:

  • Today's market isn't the same as yesterday's
  • You might have had a bad run
  • This might be a grade A play
  • Or you might be smashing it out of the park.

Same strategy, same trader.

But different context.

And often the answer isn't changing your setup…

It's adjusting your risk up or down accordingly.

So tonight at 7pm (UK) we're diving into:

Risk as a Dynamic Variable.

We'll talk about...

  • Why the same risk feels different depending on the week you're having.
  • The difference between your account being down and your confidence being down.
  • And why sometimes... increasing your size is the smartest trade you'll make all day. 🤯
